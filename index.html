Why one fire, not five burners

Fire doesn't negotiate, and neither do we.

A gas flame is the same every day. Wood isn't — it changes with the season, the rain, how long the log sat before it was split. Cooking on it means paying attention to something that won't repeat itself, which is exactly why nothing off this fire tastes quite the same twice.
